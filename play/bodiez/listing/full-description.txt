BodieZ is a strength-training log and planner that starts from what your body can do.

Tell it three things — whether you can stand or walk, use your arms, use your legs — and the exercise list shows what you can actually do. Train seated or lying down, without leg work, or without arm work. Nothing is hidden for good: change an answer any time.

What you can do
• Log every set with weight and reps, and see what you did last time
• Build routines and tag them with Mon–Sun or days you name yourself
• Log a workout after the fact, with the date it happened
• Body map: which muscles you have trained lately and which are overdue
• Goals, body-weight log, recovery log and step counts
• Progress measured against your own earlier sessions — no made-up scores

How it is different
Your progress is compared with you, not with anyone else. Ratings come from your logged numbers, and the app says what each number is built from.

BodieZ is a training log, not medical advice or therapy. Talk to your doctor or physio before starting a new exercise programme.
