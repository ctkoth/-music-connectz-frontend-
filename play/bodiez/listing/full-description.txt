🏋️ BodieZ is a workout log that starts from what your body can do.

Before it shows you a single exercise, it asks what your body can do: can you stand or walk, can you use your arms (or only one), can you use your legs (or only one). The list shrinks to what is actually possible, and it says how far:

• Seated or lying only: 60 of 90 exercises
• No legs: 42 of 90
• No arms: 29 of 90
• No arms and no legs: 4 of 90. The library is thin there, and we would rather tell you than hide it.

Nothing is hidden for good. Change an answer any time, and every past session keeps its exercises either way.

✍️ LOG A SET
Weight first, then reps, the order you say it in. BodieZ shows what you did last time on the same lift and repeats it in one tap. A rest timer counts from your last set and saves the rest you really took. It never makes one up. Forgot to log it? Log a past workout with the date it happened. It is marked as logged afterwards, and no duration or rest is invented for it.

📅 PLAN
Build routines and tag them Mon–Sun or with days you name yourself ("Leg day", "Gym with Sam"). Park them in Inbox, Today, Upcoming, Anytime or Someday. Give a routine a training goal (muscle gain, toning, slimming or strength) and it suggests sets, reps and rest for it.

🎯 GOALS
Four kinds, all worked out from what you logged: a lift at a target weight, sessions per week, total workouts, and body weight.

🧭 COACH
The coach is arithmetic, not an AI. It compares each lift with your own earlier best and says add weight, hold steady, or that a lift has been untouched for 21 days. A muscle shows "not rated yet" until a lift has two sessions to compare, because one session is not a trend.

🗺️ BODY MAP
Which muscles you trained this week and which you have not. "Overworked" counts separate training days, not sets, so five sets in one session is not five sessions.

🌙 RECOVERY AND STEPS
A daily check-in for sleep quality and fatigue, and a step count you add by hand.

📏 lb or kg, your pick.

Honest about what is missing: short demo clips exist for 37 of the 90 exercises. The other 53 have none yet.

Your progress is compared with you, not with anyone else. Nothing in BodieZ is scored by an AI, and there are no ads and nothing to buy.

BodieZ is a training log, not medical advice or therapy. Talk to your doctor or physio before starting a new exercise programme.
