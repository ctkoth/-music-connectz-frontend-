Music ConnectZ is where you make music with other people and can prove what you brought.

RECORD A TAKE, GET IT SCORED
Lay down one take — rap, vocals, guitar, bass, keys, drums or violin — and an AI coach scores it out of ten on what a single take can show: flow, timing, breath, pitch, tone, delivery. You get what worked, what to fix, and one drill to run before the next take. Record on the mic or camera, or upload a file you already have. Scores are AI-generated, and every result says what one take can and cannot show.

COLLAB WITHOUT GETTING BURNED
Bring a song, find the cover artist and the videographer, and the finished piece is ONE post that belongs to all of you. Money sits in escrow until the work lands, so nobody has to pay and pray, and nobody has to work on trust alone.

GET RATED ON WHAT YOU DID
Ratings from other members move two things: the skill you used and the piece of work itself. What you charge comes from your record, so it is something you can point at.

NO SURPRISE PRICES
Energy regenerates over time. SpinaZ come from rating, referring and offers. PromptZ pay for AI. Every action that spends something tells you what it costs before you press it.
