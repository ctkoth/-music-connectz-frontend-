Music ConnectZ is where you make music with other people and can prove what you brought🎶

I built it because credit goes to whoever presses publish — not to whoever did the work. Here, the work is the record.

RECORD A TAKE🎤, GET IT SCORED
Lay down one take — rap, vocals, guitar, bass, keys, drums or violin. An AI coach scores it out of ten on what one take can actually show: flow🌊, timing⏱️, breath🫁, pitch🎯, tone🌈, delivery🔥. You get what worked, what to fix and one drill before the next take. Mic, camera or a file you already have. The scores are AI-generated, and every result says what one take can't show.

COLLAB🤝 WITHOUT GETTING BURNED
Bring a song, find the cover artist and the videographer. The finished thing is one post that belongs to all of you — not credited to whoever pressed publish. Money sits in escrow until the work lands. Nobody pays and prays. Nobody works on trust alone.

GET RATED⭐ ON WHAT YOU DID
Every rating moves two things: the skill you used and the piece of work. What you charge comes from your record, so it's something you can point at, not something you claim.

PRICES COME FIRST💵
Energy⚡ regenerates. SpinaZ🍥 come from rating, referring and offers. PromptZ🏷️ pay for AI. Whatever an action costs, it shows before you press — a price discovered by paying it isn't a price, it's a bill. A take the coach can't read isn't charged.

NOTHING IS A DEAD END🔗
A track you record can be coached, posted, put in a battle or taken into a collab. Every screen has somewhere to go next.
