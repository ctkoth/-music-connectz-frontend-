Music ConnectZ is where you make music with other people and can prove what you brought.

RECORD A TAKE, GET IT SCORED
Lay down one take — rap, vocals, guitar, bass, keys, drums or violin — and an AI coach scores it out of ten on the things that take can actually show. Flow, timing, breath, pitch, tone, delivery. You get what worked, what to fix, and one drill to run before the next one. Record on mic or camera, or upload a file you already have.

COLLAB WITHOUT GETTING BURNED
Bring a song, find the cover artist and the videographer, and the finished thing is ONE post that belongs to all of you — not credited to whoever happened to press publish. Money sits in escrow until the work lands, so nobody has to pay and pray, and nobody has to work on trust alone.

GET RATED ON WHAT YOU DID
Every rating moves two things: the skill you used, and the piece of work itself. Your rate comes from your record, so what you charge is something you can point at instead of something you claim.

AN ECONOMY THAT SAYS ITS PRICES
Energy regenerates. SpinaZ come from rating, referring and offers. PromptZ pay for AI. Every action that spends something tells you what it costs BEFORE you press it — never after.

BADGEZ THAT DO SOMETHING
Earned from what's already in your record, never handed out. Founding Fifty doubles your Energy. The Ear raises the ratings cap that pays. Polyglot stops translation counting against your daily limit. Every badge changes a number you can point at.
