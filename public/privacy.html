Music ConnectZ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · Music ConnectZ LLC

This policy explains what information Music ConnectZ ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Music ConnectZ website (musicconnectz.net) and the Music ConnectZ Android app, to BodieZ, our fitness app (the BodieZ Android app and its website), to Sentence ConnectZ, our AI writing app (its website), and to IntelligenceZ, our app for AI writing, chat and MIDI composing and for mastering a song on your own device (the IntelligenceZ Android app and its website), and to SkillZ, our app for getting a recording of your singing, rapping or playing scored by AI (the SkillZ Android app and its website). The apps share one account. BodieZ, Sentence ConnectZ, IntelligenceZ and SkillZ have no purchases, no ads, no offerwall and no third-party sign-in, so everything below about those applies to Music ConnectZ only.

Information we collect

  • Account information you provide: username, email address, phone number (optional), birthday (optional), and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Profile & content you choose to add: display details, personas, nationalities, posts, ratings, comments, media, and messages.
  • Sign-in via third parties (optional): if you use Google, GitHub, Apple, or another provider, we receive your basic profile (id, email, name, avatar) to create or match your account.
  • Age verification (optional): if you verify you are 18+, identity checks are performed by Stripe Identity. We receive only a yes/no result and your verified age — we never store your ID document.
  • Payments: card details are entered directly with our payment processors (Stripe, PayPal) and are never seen or stored by us. We keep a record of your purchases and membership tier.
  • BodieZ (fitness) information you choose to log: workouts (the date, when they start and end, and any note you add), sets (the exercise, reps, weight, how long you rested between sets, and whether you did one with one arm or one leg), routines, goals, body-weight check-ins, recovery check-ins (soreness, sleep quality and fatigue, each rated 1 to 5, plus an optional note), steps you type in, exercises and days you name yourself, and your answers about what your body can do (that you can't stand or walk, can't use your arms, can only use one arm, can't use your legs, or can only use one leg). Your body weight, your recovery check-ins and your answers about what your body can do are health information. Anything you type into a note or a name is stored with your account, so leave out what you don't want us to hold. If you try BodieZ without an account, we store the exercise, reps and weight you enter with your IP address and a random browser identifier; that record is not tied to an account.
  • Sentence ConnectZ (AI writing) information you choose to send: the description you type of what to write, which can include a work history, names, contact details or anything else you put in; the style you pick; the kind of document (resume, cover letter, lyrics, poem, bio, caption, post, essay or agreement); and the piece the AI writes back. Each is stored with your account, and the app lists your most recent twenty. Changes you make to a piece on screen are not saved, so copy it out if you want them. If you report a piece, we store which piece, the reason you pick and your note. Leave out what you don't want us to hold.
  • IntelligenceZ information you choose to send. Write is Sentence ConnectZ, so what is listed above for it applies. Compose (Instrumental ConnectZ): the genre, instruments, tempo, key and length you pick, the optional note you add, and the loop of notes the AI writes back. Each is stored with your account, and the app lists your most recent twenty. Chat (OCC): each message you type, and the most recent turns of the conversation on your screen, are sent with it. We do not store the conversation. A reply is stored, with the message that prompted it, only if you press Save to WorkZ, or if you report it: a report names a stored reply so that a person can read all of it, so reporting keeps it first, and the screen says so before you send. Master (Mix ConnectZ): the song you pick is read and mastered on your own device and is not sent to us. If you press Save to Your masters, the mastered WAV file is uploaded and stored with your account, with a record of the title you gave it, the settings used, and measurements of the song before and after (loudness, true peak, loudness range, stereo correlation and levels across frequency bands); the app lists your most recent twenty. A reference track you add for matching, and the song you started from, are never uploaded. Download keeps the file on your device only. A saved master is not locked behind your sign-in: its link opens for anybody who has the link, because audio players cannot send a sign-in. The link is not listed anywhere for other members, but treat a saved master like an unlisted video, and download instead of saving anything you would not want opened by someone who was given the link. Every AI run also adds a line to your ledger naming the app and what the run cost, never what you wrote. Leave out what you don't want us to hold.
  • SkillZ information you choose to send. When you press Send it to the coach in SingZ, RapZ, GuitarZ, BassZ, KeyZ, ViolinZ or DrumZ, the recording you made or attached — audio, or video if you recorded on camera or attached a video — is uploaded to our server and sent for scoring, with the genre, difficulty, and range or style you picked and whether you asked for your lyrics or your mix to be rated too. On musicconnectz.net you can instead paste a link to a public YouTube video or Short (not in the standalone SkillZ app): nothing is uploaded, Google's Gemini API watches the video from its address, and we keep that address with the take's scores. Pressing Radar read on somebody else's post sends that post's recording to be read the same way, and nothing from it is kept as yours. Your browser asks for the microphone when you press Record, and for the camera only if you choose to record on camera; nothing is recorded before that, and you can attach a file instead. By default we do not store the recording. If you tick Keep this take before you send it on musicconnectz.net (the option is not in the standalone SkillZ app), the recording is saved as one of your uploads: it counts toward your storage, is private to you unless you post it or choose, when you share a take's score page, to let the recording play there (anyone with that link can then play it, until you untick it), can be deleted by you at any time, and is deleted with your account. We store, with your account, what the coach gave back for that take: the scores, what it heard, its verdict and advice, plus the genre, difficulty, range or style and date, and the range it read from the take. The coach's history of your takes is built from those, and so are the goal range, fatigue setting, tempo range and favourite rap styles you set. Each scored take also gets a score page holding the score, verdict, genre and your username; the SkillZ app does not show or share it, but its link opens for anybody who has the link, and is not listed for other members. If you report a score, we store which score, the reason you pick and any note you add; a person running the service can then read that score and what the coach wrote about it, and a report does not include the recording; a take you did not choose to keep is not stored at all. Every scored take also adds a line to your ledger naming the app and what it cost. Anything you say or sing in a take goes with it, so leave out what you don't want sent.
  • Usage & device data: basic technical data (IP address, app version, activity such as views and rewards) used to run the economy features and prevent fraud. When you open sign-up or sign-in we also record that step with a random identifier kept on your device, with no name or email, so we can see where sign-up breaks. We keep the IP address an account was created from for as long as the account exists, and the addresses of its most recent sign-ins (up to 12; ones older than 180 days are dropped the next time the account signs in), to spot duplicate accounts and abuse.

How we use your information

  • To create and operate your account and the app's features (posts, messaging, collaborations, memberships, the in-app economy).
  • To process payments, subscriptions, and rewards (Energy, SpinaZ, PromptZ).
  • To show rewarded ads and offers you choose to engage with, and to credit the rewards you earn.
  • To keep the service safe — preventing fraud, abuse, and reward farming.
  • To communicate with you about your account and, if you opt in, digests and updates.
  • In BodieZ, to run your workout log: to filter the exercise list to what you can do, and to show your progress, records, body map, coach suggestions, goals and rest suggestions, all worked out from your own entries. None of that uses an AI model or any other member's data. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.
  • The BodieZ AI coach, only on the Music ConnectZ website and only when you press it (the BodieZ app does not offer it): an AI read sends the workout, routine or muscle you picked to Google's Gemini API — the exercises, sets, reps, weights, rest and dates, your note on that workout, your earlier sessions of the same lifts, your goal names and your answers about what your body can do — so its advice fits you; a form check sends the video you choose of one set, the lift you name, your optional question, and the same goal names and answers about what your body can do. Each goes with our own instructions and without your name, username or email. We keep the result (the advice and, for a form check, its scores) with your account so you can reopen and download it, until you delete it or your account. We do not keep the video. We do not use any of it for advertising and we do not sell it.
  • We do not show your BodieZ information to other members. It is not on your profile, in member search or on any leaderboard. The one way anyone else sees any of it is if you send it yourself: after a workout, BodieZ can prefill a summary (the workout name, how long it took, total weight lifted, sets, reps and personal records) into a message or a PostZ post, or open your device's share sheet. Nothing is sent until you press send or post, and a PostZ post is public unless you change its audience.
  • In Sentence ConnectZ, to write what you ask for: the text you describe is sent to Google's Gemini API, which writes the piece and returns it. We send it with our instructions to the writer and without your name, username or email. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.
  • In SkillZ, to score your take: the recording is passed to Google's Gemini API, which listens to it and scores it; what Gemini reports about it (its scores and notes, as text) goes to Anthropic's Claude API, which checks the advice against it, together with the genre, difficulty and style you picked and a short summary of your earlier scores (the one that has been weakest lately, how one has moved, and whether recent takes read as strain). Claude is sent no audio. Each goes with our own instructions and without your name, username or email, and with the style of voice the coach writes in if you have set one. We do not use any of it for advertising and we do not sell it. If the second check is not available, you get the first notes only, and the screen says so.
  • In IntelligenceZ, to do what you ask: Write and Compose send what you describe to Google's Gemini API, and Chat sends your message and the recent turns of the conversation to Anthropic's Claude API. Each goes with our own instructions and without your name, username or email (anything you type yourself, such as a name on a resume, goes with it). We do not use any of it for advertising and we do not sell it.
  • Mastering in Mix ConnectZ uses no AI model and no server. It runs in your own browser or app: it measures the song, then applies filters, compression and limiting, and the screen lists each move beside the measurement that caused it. Nothing about the song is sent to us unless you press Save.
  • We do not show your Sentence ConnectZ pieces to other members. The ways anyone else sees one are the ones you choose in Music ConnectZ: posting it in PostZ, or attaching it to a collaboration, battle or release, which stores the text you attach with it. The Sentence ConnectZ app has neither. A person running the service can open a piece you report in order to review it.
  • We do not show your SkillZ scores to other members: they are not on your profile or in member search. The coach's leaderboards, if a coach has any drills for them to rank, show a username, a level and experience points and nothing from a take. The one way anyone else reaches a score is the score page described above, which opens for anybody who has its link; this app does not hand you that link, and the ways to share a score in Music ConnectZ (a post, a share sheet) are not in the SkillZ app.
  • We do not show your IntelligenceZ loops, chats or masters to other members, apart from the open link to a saved master described above. The ways anyone else sees one are the ones you choose in Music ConnectZ: posting it, attaching it to a collaboration, battle or release, or sharing a kept Chat reply as a post. The IntelligenceZ app has none of them. A person running the service can open a reply you report in order to review it.

Third-party services

We share the minimum data needed with providers who act on our behalf:

  • Stripe / PayPal — payments and (Stripe) age verification.
  • Google AdMob — rewarded video ads in the Music ConnectZ app. BodieZ shows no ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad delivery and measurement per Google's Privacy Policy.
  • ayeT-Studios — the OfferZ offerwall in Music ConnectZ, when you choose to complete offers.
  • OAuth providers (Google, GitHub, Apple, etc.) — only if you sign in with them on Music ConnectZ.
  • Google (Gemini API) — Sentence ConnectZ and IntelligenceZ's Compose send what you describe to Google's Gemini API to be written or composed, SkillZ sends the recording of a take to be listened to and scored, and the AI features in Music ConnectZ, such as the instrument coaches and the BodieZ AI coach (your workout logs, or a video of a set you choose to send), and Image ConnectZ (your description, the options you pick and, if you choose, one picture: one to borrow the style of, or a photo of yourself so the image keeps your likeness — that picture is used for the one image and not kept by us; the image made is kept in your account and FileZ until you delete it), LookZMaxZ (adults who have passed the ID check only: the photo you attach, or whose image address from Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, Snapchat, LinkedIn or Pinterest you paste — which our server fetches once from that site's image server — is sent to be read; a reading is of a photo you confirm is of you, while Look Inspiration takes a photo of anyone and describes only the look in it, with no score; neither the photo nor the result is kept), and Video ConnectZ (the description of each shot and, if you pick one, one of your FaceZ photos, sent to Google's Veo video model; the description is kept with the clip, and every clip and finished music video is kept in your account and FileZ until you delete it; its Develop & Direct planner, when you press it, also sends the track you picked — one of your own uploads — and any lyrics and mood you type, so the shots can be planned to the song; the plan is shown to you and not stored), use it too. Google processes what it is sent under its own terms (Gemini API terms).
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — IntelligenceZ's Chat sends your message and the recent turns of the conversation to Anthropic's Claude API to be answered, SkillZ sends the text of what Gemini reported about a take (never the recording) so the advice can be checked, and OCC in Music ConnectZ uses it too. Anthropic processes what it is sent under its own terms (Anthropic's commercial terms).
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the exercise demo clips. If you tap Demo in BodieZ, GitHub sees your IP address and browser details. No account or workout data is sent.
  • Hosting/infrastructure — Render (backend) and Vercel (web) host the service. The websites of BodieZ, Sentence ConnectZ, IntelligenceZ and SkillZ are served from their own web hosts.

Your choices & rights

  • Delete your account at any time: in BodieZ, Sentence ConnectZ, IntelligenceZ or SkillZ, scroll to the bottom of any screen and open Delete my account; in Music ConnectZ, open ProfileZ. Or follow these steps, or email us. One account covers all the apps, so it is deleted from all of them. Deleting the account first cancels any Premium, StatZ or auto top-up billing at Stripe, with no refund of the rest of a paid period; if it can't be cancelled, the account is not deleted.
  • You can delete your own routines, goals, named days and created exercises whenever you like. Logged workouts, sets, weigh-ins, recovery check-ins and step entries can only be removed by deleting the account.
  • A master you saved in Mix ConnectZ can be deleted whenever you like, from Your masters; that deletes the file as well as the record. A reply you kept from OCC can be deleted from WorkZ. Pieces Sentence ConnectZ wrote for you and loops Instrumental ConnectZ composed can only be removed by deleting the account. So can the scores SkillZ kept and their score pages; there is no recording to remove, because none is kept.
  • Edit or remove profile details and content you've posted.
  • Opt out of non-essential emails in Settings.
  • Manage ad personalization through your device's Google settings.

Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete it from our database straight away, together with what it owns: your profile, everything you logged in BodieZ and the AI reads you kept there, the pieces Sentence ConnectZ wrote for you and what you typed to ask for them, the loops Instrumental ConnectZ composed, the replies you kept from OCC, the masters you saved in Mix ConnectZ (the audio files as well as the records), the scores SkillZ kept and the score page made for each one, your posts, the messages you sent and received, the ratings you gave and received, your comments, your uploaded files and your wallet. Money in the wallet is not paid out first, so withdraw it before you delete. We do not keep a copy of your transaction history after that. Stripe and PayPal keep their own records of payments made through them, under their own obligations. A few traces stay in other members' spaces without your account attached, such as notifications that mention your username and a visit record, with its IP address, on another member's restricted post. Copies in our hosting provider's logs and backups follow that provider's own retention schedule, and anything stored in your own browser stays there until you clear it.

Children

Music ConnectZ is not directed to children under 13, and you must be 13 or older to create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Certain features (money betting and adult content) require verified 18+ status.

Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, hashed passwords, signed payment and reward callbacks). No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy; we'll revise the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
