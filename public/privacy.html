Music ConnectZ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026 · Music ConnectZ LLC

This policy explains what information Music ConnectZ ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Music ConnectZ website (musicconnectz.net) and the Music ConnectZ Android app, and to BodieZ, our fitness app (the BodieZ Android app and its website). The two apps share one account. BodieZ has no purchases, no ads, no offerwall and no third-party sign-in, so everything below about those applies to Music ConnectZ only.

Information we collect

  • Account information you provide: username, email address, phone number (optional), birthday (optional), and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Profile & content you choose to add: display details, personas, nationalities, posts, ratings, comments, media, and messages.
  • Sign-in via third parties (optional): if you use Google, GitHub, Apple, or another provider, we receive your basic profile (id, email, name, avatar) to create or match your account.
  • Age verification (optional): if you verify you are 18+, identity checks are performed by Stripe Identity. We receive only a yes/no result and your verified age — we never store your ID document.
  • Payments: card details are entered directly with our payment processors (Stripe, PayPal) and are never seen or stored by us. We keep a record of your purchases and membership tier.
  • BodieZ (fitness) information you choose to log: workouts (the date, when they start and end, and any note you add), sets (the exercise, reps, weight, how long you rested between sets, and whether you did one with one arm or one leg), routines, goals, body-weight check-ins, recovery check-ins (soreness, sleep quality and fatigue, each rated 1 to 5, plus an optional note), steps you type in, exercises and days you name yourself, and your answers about what your body can do (that you can't stand or walk, can't use your arms, can only use one arm, can't use your legs, or can only use one leg). Your body weight, your recovery check-ins and your answers about what your body can do are health information. Anything you type into a note or a name is stored with your account, so leave out what you don't want us to hold. If you try BodieZ without an account, we store the exercise, reps and weight you enter with your IP address and a random browser identifier; that record is not tied to an account.
  • Usage & device data: basic technical data (IP address, app version, activity such as views and rewards) used to run the economy features and prevent fraud. When you open sign-up or sign-in we also record that step with a random identifier kept on your device, with no name or email, so we can see where sign-up breaks. We keep the IP address an account was created from for as long as the account exists, and the addresses of its most recent sign-ins (up to 12; ones older than 180 days are dropped the next time the account signs in), to spot duplicate accounts and abuse.

How we use your information

  • To create and operate your account and the app's features (posts, messaging, collaborations, memberships, the in-app economy).
  • To process payments, subscriptions, and rewards (Energy, SpinaZ, PromptZ).
  • To show rewarded ads and offers you choose to engage with, and to credit the rewards you earn.
  • To keep the service safe — preventing fraud, abuse, and reward farming.
  • To communicate with you about your account and, if you opt in, digests and updates.
  • In BodieZ, to run your workout log: to filter the exercise list to what you can do, and to show your progress, records, body map, coach suggestions, goals and rest suggestions, all worked out from your own entries. No AI model is involved and no other member's data is used. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.
  • We do not show your BodieZ information to other members. It is not on your profile, in member search or on any leaderboard. The one way anyone else sees any of it is if you send it yourself: after a workout, BodieZ can prefill a summary (the workout name, how long it took, total weight lifted, sets, reps and personal records) into a message or a PostZ post, or open your device's share sheet. Nothing is sent until you press send or post, and a PostZ post is public unless you change its audience.

Third-party services

We share the minimum data needed with providers who act on our behalf:

  • Stripe / PayPal — payments and (Stripe) age verification.
  • Google AdMob — rewarded video ads in the Music ConnectZ app. BodieZ shows no ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad delivery and measurement per Google's Privacy Policy.
  • ayeT-Studios — the OfferZ offerwall in Music ConnectZ, when you choose to complete offers.
  • OAuth providers (Google, GitHub, Apple, etc.) — only if you sign in with them on Music ConnectZ.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the exercise demo clips. If you tap Demo in BodieZ, GitHub sees your IP address and browser details. No account or workout data is sent.
  • Hosting/infrastructure — Render (backend) and Vercel (web) host the service. BodieZ's website is served from its own web host.

Your choices & rights

  • Delete your account at any time: in BodieZ, scroll to the bottom of any screen and open Delete my account; in Music ConnectZ, open ProfileZ. Or follow these steps, or email us. One account covers both apps, so it is deleted from both. Deleting the account first cancels any Premium, StatZ or auto top-up billing at Stripe, with no refund of the rest of a paid period; if it can't be cancelled, the account is not deleted.
  • You can delete your own routines, goals, named days and created exercises whenever you like. Logged workouts, sets, weigh-ins, recovery check-ins and step entries can only be removed by deleting the account.
  • Edit or remove profile details and content you've posted.
  • Opt out of non-essential emails in Settings.
  • Manage ad personalization through your device's Google settings.

Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete it from our database straight away, together with what it owns: your profile, everything you logged in BodieZ, your posts, the messages you sent and received, the ratings you gave and received, your comments, your uploaded files and your wallet. Money in the wallet is not paid out first, so withdraw it before you delete. We do not keep a copy of your transaction history after that. Stripe and PayPal keep their own records of payments made through them, under their own obligations. A few traces stay in other members' spaces without your account attached, such as notifications that mention your username and a visit record, with its IP address, on another member's restricted post. Copies in our hosting provider's logs and backups follow that provider's own retention schedule, and anything stored in your own browser stays there until you clear it.

Children

Music ConnectZ is not directed to children under 13, and you must be 13 or older to create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Certain features (money betting and adult content) require verified 18+ status.

Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, hashed passwords, signed payment and reward callbacks). No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy; we'll revise the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
