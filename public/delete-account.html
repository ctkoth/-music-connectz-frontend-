Delete your account

Music ConnectZ LLC · applies to Music ConnectZ and BodieZ

Music ConnectZ and BodieZ share one account. Deleting it in either app deletes it in both, and it cannot be undone.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  • BodieZ: sign in, open Today, scroll to the bottom and open Delete my account. Type DELETE to confirm.
  • Music ConnectZ: sign in, open ProfileZ and choose the delete-account button at the bottom of the page.

Can't sign in?

Email support@musicconnectz.net from the address on the account, with the username you want deleted in the message. We match the address to the account before deleting anything, which is why it has to be the same one.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and everything you logged in BodieZ: workouts, sets, routines, goals, body-weight and recovery check-ins, steps, and the movement answers you gave about what you can do.
  • Your posts, uploads, messages, ratings and comments on Music ConnectZ.
  • Your wallet: Energy, SpinaZ, PromptZ and any money in it. Money is not paid out when an account is deleted, so withdraw it first.

What is kept

Limited records we must keep to meet legal or accounting obligations, such as transaction records. See the Privacy Policy.
