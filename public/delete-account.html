Delete your account

Music ConnectZ LLC · applies to Music ConnectZ and BodieZ

Music ConnectZ and BodieZ share one account. Deleting it in either app deletes it in both, and it cannot be undone.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  • BodieZ app: sign in, scroll to the bottom of any screen and open Delete my account. Type DELETE to confirm.
  • Music ConnectZ: sign in, open ProfileZ and choose the delete-account button at the bottom of the page. This is also where to go if you use BodieZ inside Music ConnectZ.

Before you delete: cancel what you pay for

Deleting the account does not cancel Premium or an auto top-up with Stripe. They keep billing after the account is gone, so cancel them first in Music ConnectZ.

Can't sign in?

Email support@musicconnectz.net from the address on the account, with the username you want deleted in the message. We have to be able to match the address to the account before deleting anything, which is why it has to be the same one.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and everything you logged in BodieZ: workouts, sets, routines, goals, body-weight and recovery check-ins, steps, and the movement answers you gave about what you can do.
  • Your posts, your uploaded files (the stored copies as well as the records), your comments, the messages you sent and received, and the ratings you gave and received.
  • Your wallet: Energy, SpinaZ, PromptZ and any money or royalties in it. Money is not paid out when an account is deleted, so withdraw it first.

What is kept

  • No copy of your transaction history. Stripe and PayPal keep their own records of payments made through them, under their own obligations.
  • A few traces inside other members' spaces, without your account attached: notifications that mention your username, and a visit record (with its IP address) on another member's restricted post.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's logs and backups, which follow that provider's own retention schedule, and anything stored in your own browser until you clear it.
