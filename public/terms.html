🎫 Who gets in

🎂 You've got to be 13 or older to make an account. That's the floor, no exceptions.

🔞 Some stuff needs verified 18+ — money betting in BattleZ, and adult content. 🪪 You verify with a government ID and a selfie through Stripe Identity, and here's the part people actually care about: we get a yes/no and your verified age. That's it. We never see the document, never store it. 🛡️

🚸 Under 18? Orientation and attraction fields don't save to your profile, whatever you type. 👀 And the app straight-up tells you it dropped them — it doesn't quietly pretend they saved. That's the difference between a wall and a lie.

🙋 One account per person. It's yours to use, not to sell, rent or hand off. 🚫

🎨 What you make stays yours — genuinely

🎼 Your tracks, your videos, your art, your writing, your code. Yours. 🤝 You give us a licence to host it, show it and send it — enough to actually run the service and put your work in front of the people you posted it for, and not one inch wider. 🙅 We don't sell your work and we don't take a cut of what you earn off this platform. Ever.

⚠️ Flip side: you need the rights to what you post. 🎧 Upload somebody else's master as your own and it comes down, and your account can go with it. That's not us being precious — it's the one thing that gets a platform like this killed. 💀

🏷️ Every action that spends something tells you what it costs before you commit — like this: −1 🏷️ +8 ⚡. ✅ And if an action fails, you shouldn't be charged for it. Where the app only bills once there's a usable result, that's on purpose, not a bug.

🤝 CollabZ + escrow — nobody pays on trust

  • 🔒 Money on a paid collab sits in escrow. It does not get fired off on a handshake.
  • ⏰ Escrow auto-releases 10 days after funding unless somebody opens a dispute. 👀 That window is the payer's last shot to flag a problem — after it, the money's gone to the person who did the work.
  • 💸 A payer holding the Patron badge cuts their own window by 7 days, floor of 3. It only kicks in when every payer on the deal holds it, and it costs the payer their own protection — nobody else's. That's what makes it safe to hand out.
  • ⚖️ We review disputes. We can release, refund or split escrowed money to settle one, and on this platform that call is final.

🏅 BadgeZ, ratings and scores — earned means earned

🎯 Badges come off what the database can already prove — deals shipped, ratings given, accounts verified. 🎁 A few are gifted, and those say "gifted" right on their face. 🚫 An earned badge never gets handed out as a favour, because the second one does, every earned badge on the platform becomes unreadable. Nobody could tell what was met and what was granted.

⏳ Some badges are temporary — they track a live number, and they lapse when it falls. 📉 They tell you that before you hold one. Losing it isn't a punishment, it's just the number moving. Earn it back and it comes straight back. 🔁

💯 Ratings measure the work. We don't sell rank, and a score you could get without actually getting good isn't a score we're interested in showing you. 🎤

📺 AdZ and OfferZ

🍥 Rewarded ads (Google AdMob) and the OfferZ offerwall (ayeT-Studios) are optional, and they pay SpinaZ. ✅ Rewards land on verified completion. 🚨 Try to farm them — bots, fake completions, a pile of alt accounts — and you forfeit the rewards and probably the account. A reward you can mint in a loop isn't a reward, it's an exploit. 🕳️

🚫 What gets you removed

  • 🎧 Posting work you don't have the rights to. Anything illegal. Sexual content involving minors — instant, permanent, reported.
  • 😤 Harassment, threats, or going after an individual.
  • 💳 Fraud: chargeback abuse, payment fraud, reward farming, ban evasion.
  • 🛠️ Attacking the service — scraping at scale, hammering rate limits, poking at other members' data.

⚖️ We can suspend or pull an account for any of that. 💵 One thing worth saying plainly: getting removed doesn't torch your Money balance — except for whatever the fraud itself produced. Your real money stays your real money. 🤝

🗑️ Deleting your account

🚪 Delete it any time in ProfileZ and your personal data goes with it. 📒 We keep some records — transactions especially — where law and accounting say we have to. ⚠️ Deleting doesn't refund a purchase that's outside the 10-day window, so refund first, then delete.
