CollabZ deals and escrow

  • Money on a paid collab is held in escrow rather than sent on trust.
  • Escrow auto-releases 10 days after funding unless a dispute is opened. That window is the payer's last moment to raise a problem.
  • A payer who holds the Patron badge shortens their own window by 7 days, to a floor of 3 — it applies only when every payer on the deal holds it, and it costs the payer their own protection, nobody else's.

Badges, ratings and scores

Badges are earned from what the database can already prove — deals shipped, ratings given, accounts verified — or, where a badge says so on its face, gifted. A badge marked earned is never handed out as a favour, because one that was would make every earned badge unreadable.

Some badges are temporary and track a live number. They lapse when the number falls, and they say so before you hold them. Losing one is not a penalty; it's the number moving.

Ratings measure the work. We do not sell rank, and a score you could get without getting good is not a score we intend to show.
