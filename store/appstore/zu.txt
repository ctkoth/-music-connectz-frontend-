🎙️ QOPHA I-TAKE, THOLA AMAPHUZU
Qopha i-take eyodwa — i-rap, izwi, isiginci, ibass, amakhibhodi, amadrum noma ivayolini — futhi umqeqeshi we-AI uyinikeza amaphuzu kwayishumi ezintweni i-take eyodwa engazibonisa ngempela. I-flow, isikhathi, ukuphefumula, ukuphakama kwezwi, ithoni, ukulethwa. Uthola okusebenzile, okufanele ukulungiswe, nokuzivocavoca okukodwa ngaphambi kwe-take elandelayo. Qopha ngemakrofoni noma ikhamera, noma layisha ifayela olinalo kakade.

🤝 ISISEBENZISANO ASIKWENZI NGOKUKHWABANISWA
Letha ingoma, thola umculi wekhava nomthwebuli wamavidiyo, futhi umsebenzi osuqediwe uyi-post ENYE eyenu nonke — hhayi yalowo ocindezele ukushicilela ngephutha. Imali ihlala ku-escrow kuze kufike umsebenzi, ngakho akekho odinga ukukhokha enethemba, noma asebenze ngokukholwa kuphela.
